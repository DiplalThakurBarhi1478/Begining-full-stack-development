DOM Manipulation
 1. selecting with id 
    document.getElementById("myId")

 2. selecting with class
    document.getElementsByClassName("myClass")

 3. selecting with tag
    document.getElementsByTagName("p")

    document.querySelector("#myId/ .myClass/ tag")
